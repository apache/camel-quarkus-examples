# Support FAQ

## Account management

### How do I reset my password?

Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter the email address registered on the
account; a reset link is sent to it within a few minutes. The link is valid for 15 minutes and can
be used once. If no email arrives, check the spam folder and make sure the address is the one
registered on the account. Accounts protected by two-factor authentication ask for the second
factor after the new password has been set.

### How do I change my registered email address?

Sign in, open "Account settings" and choose "Change email". A confirmation link is sent to the new
address; the change takes effect once the link is opened. Invoices and notifications are sent to
the new address from then on.

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?

In "Account settings", open "Security" and choose "Enable two-factor authentication". Scan the QR
code with an authenticator application and enter the generated code to confirm. Store the recovery
codes shown at the end in a safe place: they are the only way back into the account if the
authenticator device is lost.

### How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is requested in "Account settings" under "Delete account". The account is
deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days; signing in during that period
cancels the deletion. Data in shared workspaces owned by other members is not affected.

## Billing and subscriptions

### Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, as well as SEPA direct debit for customers
in the European Union. Enterprise subscriptions can be paid by invoice with 30-day payment terms.

### Where can I find my invoices?

Invoices are available in "Billing" under "Invoices" and are also emailed to the billing contact
on the day they are issued. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

### What happens if my payment fails?

A failed payment is retried three times over 10 days. During that period the subscription keeps
working and the billing contact is notified after each attempt. If every attempt fails, the
subscription is downgraded to the free plan; data is kept for 90 days.

## Technical support

### How do I contact support?

Support is reached through the "Help" menu in the application or by email at
support@acme.example. Include the account email address and, for technical problems, the
diagnostics archive created by the client.

### What is the response time?

Free plan requests are answered within 2 business days. Team subscriptions get a first response
within 8 business hours, Enterprise subscriptions within 1 hour around the clock.
