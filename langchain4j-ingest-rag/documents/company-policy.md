# Company Policy: Returns, Refunds and Exchanges

This policy applies to every order placed in the Acme web store. It explains how to return a
product, when a refund is issued and how exchanges work.

## Standard returns

Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. To be accepted, a returned item must be
unused, in its original packaging and accompanied by the order number. Returns requested after
the 30-day window are declined, except for defective items covered by the warranty section below.

To start a return, sign in to your account, open the order and choose "Return items". A prepaid
shipping label is generated for orders within the European Union and the United Kingdom. For
other destinations the return shipping cost is deducted from the refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned
item reaches our warehouse. Shipping fees paid on the original order are not refunded unless the
return is caused by our mistake. If a refund has not arrived after 10 business days, contact
support with the order number and the return tracking number.

## Exchanges

An item can be exchanged for a different size or colour of the same product within the same
30-day window. Exchanges ship free of charge once the original item has been received. To
exchange a product for a different model, return it for a refund and place a new order.

## Defective, damaged or incorrect items

Report a defective, damaged or incorrect item within 14 days of delivery through the order page.
We cover the return shipping and send a replacement, or issue a full refund including the original
shipping fees. Hardware products carry a 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects; a
warranty claim is handled by support and does not require the original packaging.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, downloadable software licences, opened consumables and personalised products cannot be
returned. Items marked "final sale" on the product page are sold without a right of return.

## Gift returns

A product received as a gift can be returned within the standard 30 days. The refund is issued as
store credit to the person returning the item, so the buyer is not notified.
